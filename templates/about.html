01 The project

What is Expense Manager?

Expense Manager started as a simple way to track everyday income and expenses and grew into a complete personal-finance platform.

The goal is to help users understand where their money goes, control spending, plan budgets, manage recurring payments and make better financial decisions without making finance feel complicated.

02 Philosophy

Simple on the surface.

Financial software can become complicated quickly. Expense Manager focuses on keeping powerful features behind a clean and easy interface.
